Walnut machines cleanly and finishes well, but its movement across the grain means wide solid panels should be avoided. Veneer on a stable core is the preferred route for shutter fronts, with solid timber reserved for rails, stiles and exposed edges.

Store at 8 – 10% moisture content. Acclimatise on site for 72 hours before machining in the workshop, and re-check the moisture meter reading on every board before the cutting list is released.
