# Phase 17: what's new

**Sales and CRM**
- **Lead tracker** built on your real sheet: company, designation, location, source, business line (Furniture / Interior / Project), assigned to, quotation status, last contact, action, meeting, note. Monthly counters; overdue rows show in red; follow-ups and an activity log on each lead.
- **Pipeline** and a **Client 360** view: one client page with all their enquiries, quotations, invoices, projects, payments and history.
- **Lead and invoice import** with a preview first; duplicates are skipped.
- **Quotation builder** with a live preview: 5 type presets (Fit-out, Renovation, Interior, Proposal, Furniture) and both **single-page** and **project-based** quotations (summary page plus trade tabs, area and per-sft, Approved-by block). Templates can be imported and exported. New A4 header, footer and signature design.
- **Invoice tracker**: WF-10050… series for Furniture; PO#, received, balance, status, delivery date (red when late); monthly totals bar. Standalone invoices.
- **Projects table**: milestones (must total 100%), and an invoice for each milestone.
- **Banks, wallets and signatories** on documents (Alfalah for WI, Meezan for WF, JazzCash / Easypaisa).
- **Bookings**: public page `/book-a-visit/` with 60-min slots (Mon–Sat 10:00–7:30). Each booking creates a lead; double bookings are blocked; admin calendar and settings.
- WhatsApp payment reminders and offers, synced with leads and invoices.

**Website content**
- **Header and footer builder** (colours, import/export); **section import** (HTML / JSON / pick from a page); **section creator** with categories and tags.
- **Page templates** for articles, portfolio studies and city pages: hero style, contents list, section order, live preview, apply to existing pages (backed up first), starter templates, import/export.
- **City pages list fix** (all city pages now show).
- **AI agent (MCP)**: 5 new tools to list content and templates, create or update article and portfolio drafts, and create city drafts. They never publish.
- **FAQ groups**: link a group to articles; editing it updates every linked live page; "used on" list; duplicate.
- **Testimonials**: city field; **5 designs** (Cards, Spotlight, Slider, Wall, Navy band), chosen per page, with a live preview.
- **Media library**: stats bar, grid/list view, type filter and sort, bulk copy URLs, bulk move to folder (unused uploads only), and crop & resize saved as a new WebP copy.
